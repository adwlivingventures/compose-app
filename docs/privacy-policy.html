Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 8, 2026

Compose is built on a simple principle: what happens in this app stays on your phone.

The short version

  • We do not collect your personal data. No account, no email, no sign-up.
  • Everything you enter stays on your device. Your answers, scores, logs, and progress are stored locally on your phone and nowhere else.
  • We cannot read your data. There is no server that receives it, and no database of users.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple. We never see or store your payment details.
  • One optional exception, with your consent: anonymous milestone counts (for example, "a Day 40 session was completed") — only if you say yes during setup, and never your words, your name, or anything that identifies you. Decline and nothing is ever sent.

What stays on your device

All of the following is stored only in your phone's local storage and, where sensitive, in your phone's encrypted keychain:

  • Your onboarding answers and first name
  • Your daily progress, session completions, and streaks
  • Your self-rated control scores and habit check-ins
  • Anything you write in the app's logs and journals

None of this is uploaded, synced, backed up to our servers, or shared with anyone — because there are no servers to send it to. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it.

What leaves your device

Three narrow categories — two standard to any paid iOS app, one entirely optional:

  1. Purchase processing (Apple and RevenueCat). When you buy a Compose membership, the transaction is processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat, a purchase-infrastructure service, to validate purchases and manage access. RevenueCat receives an anonymous, randomly generated identifier and purchase receipt data — never your name, never anything you've entered in the app. RevenueCat's privacy policy is available at revenuecat.com/privacy.
  2. App Store operation. Apple may collect standard diagnostic and usage information under your own iOS settings and Apple's privacy policy, which we do not control and do not receive.
  3. Anonymous milestone counts — only if you consent. During setup, Compose asks one plain question: may we count anonymous milestones to show the method works? If you say yes, the app sends event counts such as "a session was completed on Day 40," "a composure score of 62 was measured," or "the SOS tool was opened." These events carry a number and an event name — never your name, never a written entry, never your answers, never an account or device identifier. There is no profile of you to link them to. If you decline, nothing is sent — ever — and every feature of the app works exactly the same. Events are checked against a fixed whitelist in the app's code; anything outside it is discarded before it can be sent.

We use no advertising SDKs, no tracking, and no third-party data sharing of any kind. The optional milestone counting above is the only measurement that exists.

What we never do

  • We never sell data. There is nothing to sell.
  • We never ask for your microphone, camera, contacts, or location. The app requests no such permissions.
  • We never send your name or any content you write to any server — including if you opt in to milestone counts.
  • We never use your data to train AI models. All content in the app is pre-written by our clinical team, not generated from user data.

Notifications

If a future version of Compose offers reminders, they will be optional, generated on your device, and worded neutrally. You can disable them at any time in iOS Settings.

Age

Compose is intended for adults aged 18 and over.

Not medical care

Compose is a wellness and education program. It does not collect health data as defined by HealthKit and is not connected to any health record system. See our Terms of Use for the full wellness disclaimer.

Changes to this policy

If we ever change how the app handles data — for example, if a future feature offered optional cloud backup — we will update this policy, change the effective date above, and the app will ask for your explicit consent before any data leaves your device.
