# COMPOSE — Regulation Library: 15 Curated Practices

**Purpose:** Source material for the Resources / SOS layer. Each entry maps to a nervous-system mechanism and a slot in the 75-Day Protocol. Use these three ways: (1) immediate reference links, (2) blueprints for authoring COMPOSE's own versioned in-app audio (per the deterministic-content constraint in CLAUDE.md §7), (3) clinical grounding for scripts.

## A. Guided Breathwork (fast-acting sympathetic down-regulation)

### 1. Physiological Sigh / Cyclic Sighing — 5 min
- **Links:** [Free paced timer (breathwork.tools)](https://breathwork.tools/physiological-sigh) · [Clinical summary (Physiopedia)](https://www.physio-pedia.com/Cyclic_Sighing) · [5-min guided video (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8GTd3u_qwGA)
- **Mechanism:** Double inhale re-inflates collapsed alveoli; extended exhale slows heart rate via the vagal brake. Stanford (Balban & Spiegel, 2023) found 5 min/day of cyclic sighing outperformed mindfulness meditation for mood and resting respiratory rate.
- **COMPOSE slot:** The SOS button's default sequence. Fastest known volitional down-regulator — exactly what an acute pre-intimacy anxiety spike needs.

### 2. Box Breathing (4-4-4-4) — 3–5 min
- **Links:** [QuietKit free guided tool](https://quietkit.com/box-breathing/) · [5-min beginner-pace video (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oN8xV3Kb5-Q) · [Visual animation](https://lassebomh.github.io/box-breathing/)
- **Mechanism:** Equal-ratio breathing with holds interrupts spiraling respiratory rate and gives the prefrontal cortex a counting task that competes with spectatoring cognition (attentional displacement).
- **COMPOSE slot:** Phase 1 daily conditioning warm-up. The "Navy SEAL" provenance is also a masculinity-congruent frame for this demographic — regulation as tactical skill, not wellness fluff.

### 3. Coherent / Resonance Breathing (~5.5 bpm) — 5–10 min
- **Links:** [Free coherent-breathing timer](https://deepbreathingexercises.com/breathe/coherent) · [Resonance timer (breathwork.tools)](https://breathwork.tools/resonance-breathing) · [HRV mechanism explainer](https://breathball.com/heart-rate-measurement-and-hrv/heart-coherent-breathing-the-link-between-breathing-and-heart-rate-variability-hrv/)
- **Mechanism:** Breathing at resonance frequency maximizes heart-rate variability and baroreflex gain — the trainable substrate of vagal tone. This is the *chronic* adaptation practice (raises baseline), where the sigh is the *acute* tool.
- **COMPOSE slot:** Phase 1 core daily practice; pairs perfectly with the existing paced contract/release track since it's already inhale/exhale-cued.

### 4. Nervous System Reset — 22-min Guided Breathwork
- **Link:** [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qlTC2HBmPeM)
- **Mechanism:** Longer conscious-connected format for deeper parasympathetic rebound after sustained stress.
- **COMPOSE slot:** Optional weekend "deep session" — Act III maintenance cadence.

### 5. 12-Minute Morning Breathwork Routine
- **Link:** [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HSxLK_MkaDE)
- **Mechanism:** Morning sympathetic/parasympathetic sequencing; stacks with the Vitality Habit checklist item (morning light) for habit-anchoring.
- **COMPOSE slot:** Phase 2–3 optional add-on for users asking for "more."

### 16. 4-7-8 Breath (Weil Method) — 2–5 min
- **Links:** [Dr. Weil demonstration video](https://www.drweil.com/videos-features/videos/the-4-7-8-breath-health-benefits-demonstration/) · [Official instructions](https://www.drweil.com/health-wellness/body-mind-spirit/stress-anxiety/three-breathing-exercises-and-techniques/) · [Clinical handout (Arizona Center for Integrative Medicine, PDF)](https://nursing.rutgers.edu/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/Dr.-Weil-4-7-8-Breathing-Exercise.pdf) · [12-min guided version (Mindful.org)](https://www.mindful.org/a-12-minute-4-7-8-breathing-meditation/)
- **Mechanism:** 4-count inhale, 7-count hold, 8-count audible exhale. The long hold+exhale combination drives CO2 tolerance and a strong vagal response; best evidence as a pre-sleep down-regulator.
- **COMPOSE slot:** Sleep on-ramp — supports the Vitality Habit pillar (protected sleep).

### 17. Extended Exhale (2:1 Ratio) — 3–10 min
- **Links:** [Free 4-8 paced timer (breathwork.tools)](https://breathwork.tools/extended-exhale) · [Vagal mechanism explainer (Psychology Today)](https://www.psychologytoday.com/us/blog/the-athletes-way/201905/longer-exhalations-are-an-easy-way-to-hack-your-vagus-nerve)
- **Mechanism:** Exhale twice as long as the inhale (4 in / 8 out). The purest expression of the exhale-side vagal brake; gentler entry than the double-inhale sigh for users who feel air hunger.
- **COMPOSE slot:** Alternative SOS pattern; also the bridge skill into the 4s/6s Pelvic Pacer cadence.

### 18. Humming Breath (Bhramari / "Voo") — 3–5 min
- **Links:** [Guided humming bee breath (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Qve8pvadOLg) · [Evidence review (Doctor Yogi)](https://www.doctor-yogi.com/yoga-anatomy-blog/2026/2/22/bhramari-breathing-what-the-science-really-says-about-bee-breath) · [Free Voo-breath mini-course (SE lineage)](https://www.everythingifs.com/post/academy-se-grounding-orienting-voo-breath-free-course)
- **Mechanism:** Low-pitch humming on the exhale vibrates the laryngeal branch of the vagus and dramatically boosts nasal nitric oxide production — directly on-thesis for a product whose physiology runs through NO-mediated vasodilation. Levine uses the "Voo" variant clinically in Somatic Experiencing.
- **COMPOSE slot:** Phase 1–2; strong candidate for an in-house track because the mechanism story is pure COMPOSE.

## B. Guided Meditation (interoception + attention training)

### 6. UCLA Mindful (MARC) — Free Guided Meditations
- **Links:** [Audio library](https://www.uclahealth.org/marc/audio) · [Guided meditations index](https://www.uclahealth.org/uclamindful/guided-meditations)
- **What's there:** 3–19 min tracks: Breathing Meditation, Body Scan, Body & Sound, Working with Difficulties — in English + Spanish, from an academic medical center.
- **Mechanism:** Body-scan practice is the direct clinical antidote to spectatoring: it trains attention *into* interoceptive sensation instead of the third-person evaluative view. Institutional credibility also matters for a skeptical male 18–45 buyer.
- **COMPOSE slot:** Phase 1 (short body scan) and Phase 2 (working with difficulties = defusion-adjacent).

### 7. Huberman NSDR — 10 min & 20 min
- **Links:** [10-min NSDR (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KHIbgSN2qAU) · [20-min NSDR (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hEypv90GzDE) · [NSDR hub](https://www.hubermanlab.com/nsdr)
- **Mechanism:** Yoga-nidra-derived protocol; long-exhale + body-scan + intentional relaxation produces measurable autonomic down-shift. Critically: male-voiced, science-framed, zero spiritual packaging — the lowest-resistance entry point for men who would bounce off "meditation."
- **COMPOSE slot:** Phase 1 flagship "get out of your head, into your body" practice; also the post-session recovery tool in Phase 2 exposure work.

### 8. Tara Brach — Body Scan & Grounding Library
- **Links:** [Guided meditation library](https://www.tarabrach.com/guided-meditations/) · [Basic Body Scan](https://mindfulnessexercises.com/downloads/basic-body-scan/)
- **Mechanism:** RAIN-style self-compassion framing counters the shame loop (§3 user profile: embarrassed, private). Shame is sympathetic fuel; compassion-based practice is the documented off-ramp.
- **COMPOSE slot:** Phase 3 identity consolidation — the self-relationship layer.

### 9. Diana Winston — Streaming Meditations (no download)
- **Link:** [dianawinston.com/meditations](https://dianawinston.com/meditations)
- **Mechanism:** Same MARC lineage, includes "natural awareness" practices — effortless-attention style that suits users who report "trying too hard" (performance mindset showing up *inside* the meditation).
- **COMPOSE slot:** Phase 2 — presence-over-performance mindset transfer.

### 10. Dustienne Miller — "Releasing Tension in the Pelvic Floor" (Insight Timer)
- **Link:** [Insight Timer](https://insighttimer.com/dustiennemiller/guided-meditations/pelvic-floor-meditation)
- **Mechanism:** The category bridge — a meditation whose object of attention *is* the pelvic floor, by a board-certified women's/pelvic health PT (content is applicable to men). Trains interoceptive precision exactly where COMPOSE needs it.
- **COMPOSE slot:** Phase 1, directly before the Physical Conditioning Track — primes the sensory map the reverse-kegel work depends on.

### 19. Self-Compassion Break (Kristin Neff) — 5 min
- **Links:** [Official guided audio (self-compassion.org)](https://self-compassion.org/practices/general-self-compassion-break-2/) · [Guided video (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=A7tfP1WH3_I) · [Practice guide (Greater Good, Berkeley)](https://ggia.berkeley.edu/practice/self_compassion_break)
- **Mechanism:** Three-move protocol (mindfulness of the pain → common humanity → kind self-address). The direct clinical counter to the shame loop — shame is sympathetic fuel, and this population's core state is private embarrassment. Strong RCT evidence base (Neff & Germer, MSC program).
- **COMPOSE slot:** Post-setback ecosystem, alongside the SOS Post-Setback Protocol; Phase 3 self-relationship work.

### 20. Noting / Open-Monitoring Practice — 5–10 min
- **Links:** [Noting practice audio (self-compassion.org)](https://self-compassion.org/practices/noting-practice-2/) · [Technique explainer (Mindworks)](https://mindworks.org/blog/noting-meditation-practice/) · [UCSD Center for Integrative Health guided audio library](https://cih.ucsd.edu/mindfulness/guided-audio-video)
- **Mechanism:** Label the thought ("evaluating," "worrying"), return to sensation. This is the micro-skill underneath the Thought Restructurer, trained as a standalone attention practice — metacognitive awareness without content engagement.
- **COMPOSE slot:** Phase 2 cognitive defusion foundation.

### 21. Sleep-Length Yoga Nidra — 30–45 min
- **Links:** [30-min yoga nidra, Ally Boothroyd (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8mM5Oks8yZc) · [30-min NSDR-style nidra](https://allyboothroyd.com/30-minute-non-sleep-deep-rest-yoga-nidra/)
- **Mechanism:** Long-form rotation-of-consciousness protocol; the extended cousin of the 10-min Deep Drop for full unwind or sleep onset.
- **COMPOSE slot:** Act II/III maintenance content and the nighttime usage window (high-retention surface for this demographic).

## C. Somatic & Pelvic Floor Drop Practices (bottom-up release)

### 11. Michelle Kenway (Physiotherapist) — Guided Pelvic Floor Relaxation for Men
- **Links:** [Guided relaxation for men](https://www.pelvicexercises.com.au/pelvic-floor-relaxation-for-men/) · [Relaxation stretches + breathing](https://www.pelvicexercises.com.au/pelvic-floor-relaxation-stretches/) · [YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/user/MichelleKenway/videos)
- **Mechanism:** Physio-led, male-specific downtraining: diaphragmatic breath → pelvic bulge/release → positional stretches (child's pose, deep squat). Overactive pelvic floor is a documented contributor to PE and pelvic pain; downtraining is the evidence-based counter.
- **COMPOSE slot:** The gold-standard reference for the entire Physical Conditioning Track's release half.

### 12. University of Calgary — Pelvic Floor Drops / Reverse Kegels Protocol (PDF)
- **Link:** [Clinical handout (PDF)](https://cumming.ucalgary.ca/sites/default/files/teams/88/Reverse%20Kegels%20or%20PF_Drops.pdf)
- **Mechanism:** Step-by-step clinical instructions for the drop: inhale into the belly, allow the pelvic floor to descend/"let go," no pushing. Academic-medical provenance = safe script blueprint.
- **COMPOSE slot:** Source document for authoring COMPOSE's own drop-cue audio scripts.

### 13. Pelvic Health Solutions — Reverse Kegels (Pelvic Floor Drops) Guide
- **Links:** [Technique guide](https://pelvichealthsolutions.ca/knowledge-base/reverse-kegels-pelvic-floor-drops) · [Reverse-kegel overview (Pelvic Health NJ)](https://www.pelvichealthnj.com/pelvic-floor-blog/understanding-reverse-kegels-the-other-half-of-pelvic-floor-health/)
- **Mechanism:** Reinforces the contract/relax asymmetry most men get wrong (everyone kegels; almost no one drops). Framing "the other half of pelvic floor health" is strong in-app copy territory.
- **COMPOSE slot:** Phase 1 education layer + conditioning-track cue language.

### 14. Peter Levine / Somatic Experiencing — Free Experiential Exercises
- **Links:** [Ergos Institute free exercises](https://www.somaticexperiencing.com/experiential) · [Pendulation demo with Levine (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BTmY5-TvXfI) · [Free grounding/orienting/Voo mini-course](https://www.everythingifs.com/post/academy-se-grounding-orienting-voo-breath-free-course)
- **Mechanism:** Pendulation (oscillating attention between activation and calm) is functionally the same skill as Phase 2's arousal-tolerance work: stay present with rising activation without escaping into the head. Orienting resets the threat-scan; Voo breath vibrates the vagus.
- **COMPOSE slot:** Phase 2 Exposure & Mastery — the somatic vocabulary for high-arousal tolerance.

### 15. Pelvic/Diaphragmatic Breathing + Men's Pelvic Pain Downtraining (video set)
- **Links:** [Pelvic (diaphragmatic) breathing for pelvic floor relaxation (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3XnFyXPiEHw) · [Breathing and the pelvic floor (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PJprXsSvdag) · [Pelvic floor relaxation for men with chronic pelvic pain (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Fac5ihuQ1Ws) · [Diaphragm–pelvic floor mechanics explainer (Hinge Health)](https://www.hingehealth.com/resources/articles/diaphragmatic-breathing-pelvic-health/)
- **Mechanism:** The diaphragm and pelvic floor move as a coupled piston — inhale descends both. Teaching the breath-pelvis link is the prerequisite skill for every drop cue in the app.
- **COMPOSE slot:** Day 1–7 foundation content; the "aha" that makes the rest of the conditioning track make sense.

### 22. Progressive Muscle Relaxation (Jacobson PMR) — 10–15 min
- **Links:** [Guided audio (Dartmouth Student Wellness)](https://students.dartmouth.edu/wellness-center/wellness-mindfulness/mindfulness-meditation/guided-recordings/progressive-muscle-relaxation) · [Guided audio (Therapist Aid)](https://www.therapistaid.com/audio/progressive-muscle-relaxation-audio) · [Guided exercises (U. Melbourne Counselling)](https://services.unimelb.edu.au/counsel/resources/guided-exercises/progressive-muscle-relaxation)
- **Mechanism:** Systematic tense-then-release teaches the felt *contrast* between clenched and released — generalizing body-wide the exact interoceptive lesson the pelvic drop teaches locally. One of the oldest, best-evidenced somatic protocols in clinical psychology.
- **COMPOSE slot:** Phase 1; the whole-body companion to pelvic down-training.

### 23. 5-4-3-2-1 Sensory Grounding — 2–5 min
- **Links:** [Clinical overview (Therapist Aid)](https://www.therapistaid.com/therapy-article/grounding-techniques-article) · [Guided audio (Insight Timer)](https://insighttimer.com/themindfulcompass/guided-meditations/5-4-3-2-1-grounding-practice-for-worry-and-anxiety) · [Therapist-guided audio (DHC Talking Therapies)](https://dhctalkingtherapies.co.uk/therapist-guided-audio-exercise-grounding-technique/)
- **Mechanism:** Five things seen, four felt, three heard, two smelled, one tasted — exteroceptive anchoring that works *eyes-open, mid-situation*, when audio-guided breathing isn't practical.
- **COMPOSE slot:** SOS ecosystem — the silent, in-the-moment variant; teachable as a card, not just a track.

### 24. Down-Training Stretch Sequence — 10 min
- **Links:** [Pelvic floor relaxation stretches + breathing (Kenway)](https://www.pelvicexercises.com.au/pelvic-floor-relaxation-stretches/) · [U Calgary drops protocol (PDF)](https://cumming.ucalgary.ca/sites/default/files/teams/88/Reverse%20Kegels%20or%20PF_Drops.pdf)
- **Mechanism:** Breath-paced happy baby, butterfly, bridges, alternating pelvic tilts — the exact supporting stretches the clinical canon prescribes (§3), with glutes/abs kept soft. Positional opening amplifies what the breathing alone starts.
- **COMPOSE slot:** Phase 1–2 evening routine; guided-video candidate for the conditioning track's "long day" variant.

## D. Mind / Rewire (top-down retraining — CBST, defusion, identity)

**Doctrine constraints (binding):** feeling-paired audio only — bare-text affirmation walls are banned (§7.6); first-person copy must be Level III ("I no longer") or Level IV ("I am"); field POV only; every Mind track opens with a ~60-second breath on-ramp (state before statement, §7.1). This category is the surfacing of the CBST modality (canon §2.2) and the Psychological Foundations doctrine as practice content.

### 25. Cognitive Defusion — "Leaves on a Stream" (ACT)
- **Links:** [Free guided audio (Psychwire — Russ Harris)](https://psychwire.com/free-resources/audio/resource-efrgrk/leaves-on-a-stream) · [Guided audio (Therapist Aid)](https://www.therapistaid.com/audio/leaves-on-a-stream) · [Exercise script (Mindfulness Muse, PDF)](https://www.mindfulnessmuse.com/wp-content/uploads/2011/09/Cognitive-Defusion-Exercise-1.pdf)
- **Mechanism:** Thoughts placed on leaves and watched floating past — trains the observer-of-thoughts stance (defusion) without engaging content. The standalone version of the skill the Thought Restructurer applies in crisis. Core ACT evidence base.
- **COMPOSE slot:** Phase 2 flagship Mind track.

### 26. Guided Reframe Sessions (in-house)
- **Source material:** the Thought Restructurer's authored reframes + `docs/COMPOSE-Phase-{1,2,3}-Scripts.md`.
- **Mechanism:** The strongest Restructurer reframes, spoken over breath pacing rather than read from a screen — audio impression beats text (§7.6), and hearing the reframe in the app's voice builds the authority loop.
- **COMPOSE slot:** Phase 2–3; pure in-house authoring, no external source needed.

### 27. Identity Rehearsal (in-house, field POV)
- **Source material:** `docs/research/PSYCHOLOGICAL-FOUNDATIONS.md` (register arc, field-POV law).
- **Mechanism:** Guided first-person future rehearsal — through his own eyes and skin, never watching himself (observer POV is spectatoring training and is banned). The legitimate clinical form of "visualization": mental rehearsal on a regulated nervous system.
- **COMPOSE slot:** Phase 3 identity consolidation; the "I am" register lives here, feeling-paired.

### 28. Evening Evidence Review (in-house)
- **Source material:** votes-not-verdicts doctrine (§7.8).
- **Mechanism:** A 3-minute guided end-of-day review — count today's votes (session done, presence practiced, focus kept), impress them as identity evidence, close the day without self-grading. Operationalizes the retention doctrine as a practice.
- **COMPOSE slot:** Phase 3 + Act II/III maintenance cadence.

## E. Deliberately Excluded (and why — this is authority-building content)

- **Wim Hof / cyclic hyperventilation:** deliberately *sympathomimetic* — it raises adrenaline. An activation practice inside a down-regulation product is a clinical self-contradiction for this population. (Also a strong in-app one-liner and content-channel angle: "why the most famous breathwork on the internet is the wrong tool for you.")
- **TRE (trauma-release tremoring):** thin evidence base; inducing neurogenic tremors in unsupervised, already-anxious solo users is a support-ticket and scare risk.
- **Standard Kegels as default:** prescribing contractions to a hypertonic floor worsens the problem (canon §2.1); strengthening work exists only behind the hypotonic diagnostic branch.
- **Mid-stream urine stopping:** bladder/UTI risk; banned as an instruction anywhere in the product (canon §3).
- **Angion Method and traction/force techniques:** zero peer-reviewed validation, tissue-damage risk (canon §6); COMPOSE positions as the safe clinical alternative.
- **Bare-text affirmations:** Level II self-talk restates deficit and text-only impression decays into content; all identity material ships feeling-paired as audio (§7.6).

## Licensing & Integration Notes (important)

1. **Linking ≠ licensing.** Linking out or using YouTube's official embed is legal. Downloading, re-hosting, or re-voicing someone's recorded audio inside the app is infringement.
2. **Techniques aren't copyrightable; recordings and scripts are.** Box breathing, cyclic sighing, reverse kegels, body scanning — the methods are free to use. Author original scripts (grounded in the clinical sources above), record original VO, version it in the content pipeline. This is also what CLAUDE.md §7 (deterministic, authored content) already requires.
3. **Third-party embeds break the product's own rules.** YouTube embeds inject ads, branding, autoplay "related videos" with clinical/awkward thumbnails, and leave a watch-history trail — a discretion violation for this user (§3 lock-screen shame extends to browser/YouTube history) and an aesthetic violation of Ember Dusk. Treat external links as a stopgap, not the destination.
4. **UCLA MARC / Tara Brach / Kenway content** is free to *stream from their properties*, not free-license. If a genuine in-app partnership is ever wanted, MARC and individual PTs (Kenway, Miller) do license content — but COMPOSE's moat is owning its own audio.
