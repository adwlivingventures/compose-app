## Distribution decisions (settled — don't relitigate)
1. **Faceless, AI-assisted content** on TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts.
   2–3 accounts per platform (not 10 — concentrate learning), 2–3 posts/day.
2. **Hard line: no synthetic testimonials.** No AI-generated person ever claims a
   personal result. (FTC fake-endorsement rule covers AI testimonials; also brand
   poison for an honesty-positioned product.) AI-assisted production of
   psychoeducation/demos is fine.
3. **Reddit = the authentic channel.** Genuine founder participation in
   r/PrematureEjaculation, r/erectiledysfunction, r/sex — value-first, no astroturfing.
4. **Category-enemy framing, never named competitors:** pills, numbing sprays,
   willpower myths. No mention of Mojo/The Coach/etc. anywhere.

## Audience mechanics that shape every clip
- **Engagement shame:** this audience will not like, comment, or share. Optimize for
  watch time and profile→link clicks, not engagement. Judge formats accordingly.
- **Go one level up:** performance anxiety, nervous-system training, "why your body
  betrays you under pressure" — not explicit sexual content (platform moderation
  suppresses it; the landing page says the quiet part).
- **Proven demoable assets:** onboarding quiz → Composure Score reveal (hooky,
  screen-records beautifully), breathing orb ("watch this drop your heart rate in 60
  seconds"), Ember Dusk aesthetic itself.
- Social proof is structurally muted for this app (users won't review or share) —
  content has to do the work referrals normally do.
