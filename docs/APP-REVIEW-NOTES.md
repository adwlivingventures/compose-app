Compose is a men's wellness and mindfulness-training app focused on calm, presence, and
confidence around intimacy. Context that may help your review:

1. CONTENT: All content is pre-written, versioned, and bundled in the binary — audio
   tracks, lessons, and exercises are authored wellness/education material. There is no
   user-generated content, no community features, no chat, and no live or generative AI
   output anywhere in the app. There is no nudity, no explicit or erotic content, and no
   explicit language; content is comparable in tone to meditation and breathwork apps.

2. HEALTH CLAIMS: The app makes no medical or treatment claims. It does not diagnose,
   treat, or claim to cure any condition, and in-app content encourages users to consult
   a physician where physical causes may be involved. It is a wellness/education product,
   not a medical device or telehealth service.

3. PRIVACY: All personal data (questionnaire answers, daily self-ratings, progress) is
   stored locally on-device only and never transmitted. The only network events are
   optional, consent-based, anonymous milestone counts (e.g., "a day was completed") with
   no identifiers and no user content. Notifications are deliberately generic for user
   privacy.

4. MONETIZATION: A single auto-renewable membership (annual primary, monthly option) via
   in-app purchase, presented after onboarding with full price and renewal disclosure.
   No trials, no consumables, no external purchase links.
