# Privacy / data questions

**Use when:** "What data do you collect?" / "Can anyone see what I do in the app?" /
"Is this on your servers?" This user is often deciding whether to trust the app at all —
answer with specifics, not reassurance-speak.

---

Subject: Re: Your privacy question

Hi [first name],

This is the right question to ask, and I can give you an unusually clean answer, because
we designed for exactly this concern.

Everything personal stays on your phone. Your quiz answers, your daily scores, your
progress, anything you log in the app — all of it is stored locally on your device and
never uploaded to our servers. We couldn't read it if we wanted to, and neither can
anyone else who doesn't have your unlocked phone.

The one thing the app can send — and only if you said yes during setup — is anonymous
milestone counts, like "someone completed Day 12." No name, no email, no answers, no
written content, ever. It exists so we can see whether the program works in aggregate.
If you declined, nothing is sent at all, and the app works identically either way. You
can check what you chose in the app's settings.

Beyond the app itself: notifications are written so they'd mean nothing to anyone
glancing at your lock screen, and your purchase appears on statements as an Apple charge
like any other app.

Full details are in the privacy policy inside the app, but that's the whole of it — no
fine-print surprises.

— Compose Support
