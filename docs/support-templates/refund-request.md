# Refund request

**Use when:** any refund ask — polite, angry, or mid-complaint. Same template either way.
**Rule:** we never promise, deny, predict, or argue a refund. Apple decides all App Store
refunds; our only job is a warm, fast pointer to their process.

---

Subject: Re: Your Compose purchase

Hi [first name],

Thanks for reaching out, and no hard feelings at all — this should be simple to sort out.

All billing for Compose runs through Apple, so refund requests go directly to them rather
than through us (developers aren't able to process or influence them). Here's the fastest
path:

1. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with your Apple ID
2. Find the Compose charge and choose "Request a refund"
3. Apple usually responds within 24–48 hours

If you're stepping away, I'd only ask one thing: if there was something about the program
that didn't land for you, I'd genuinely value hearing it. It shapes what we build.

Whatever you decide, I hope the time you spent with the practice serves you.

— Compose Support
