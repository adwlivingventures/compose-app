# Bug / technical issue

**Use when:** crashes, audio not playing, purchase not unlocking, lost progress.
**Note:** progress is stored on-device, so lost progress from a deleted app is usually
unrecoverable — be straight about that. A purchase not unlocking is almost always fixed
by Restore Purchases.

Sorry about this — let's get you back on track quickly.

[Purchase not unlocking:]
Open the app, go to the You tab, and tap "Restore Purchases" — this re-checks your
membership with Apple and fixes this in almost every case. Make sure you're signed into
the same Apple ID that made the purchase.

[Audio/crash issues:]
Try these in order — in our experience one of the first two resolves most cases:
1. Force-close the app and reopen it
2. Restart your phone
3. Check for an app update in the App Store
4. Confirm your ringer/media volume and that silent mode isn't muting playback

[Lost progress after deleting the app:]
I have to be honest here: your progress is stored only on your device — that's a
deliberate privacy decision, so nothing personal ever touches our servers. The trade-off
is that deleting the app deletes the record, and we genuinely have no copy to restore.
Your membership itself is safe (Restore Purchases brings it back), and the training your
nervous system has done is not lost with the log. Many men treat a restart of the early
days as consolidation — the second pass moves faster.

If none of this sorts it, reply with your iPhone model, iOS version, and what you saw
happen, and I'll dig in.
