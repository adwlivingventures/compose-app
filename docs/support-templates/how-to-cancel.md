# How to cancel

**Use when:** a user asks how to cancel. No retention tactics, no guilt, no "are you sure?"
The exit experience is part of the brand — a man who leaves cleanly speaks well of us.

---

Subject: Re: Cancelling your membership

Hi [first name],

Of course — here's how, takes under a minute:

1. Open Settings on your iPhone and tap your name at the top
2. Tap "Subscriptions"
3. Select Compose and tap "Cancel Subscription"

You'll keep full access until the end of your current billing period, and nothing renews
after that. Your progress data lives on your device, so it stays yours either way.

but no pressure at all.
