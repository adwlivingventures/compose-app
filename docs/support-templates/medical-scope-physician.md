Compose is a wellness and training program, not a medical service — so I'm not able to

conversation for them.

A practical note: if you notice things like consistently absent morning arousal, or the
difficulty is present in every context regardless of stress, that pattern is worth
mentioning to a doctor — those can point to physical contributors that training alone
doesn't address. The two approaches aren't in competition; plenty of men work on both
tracks at once.
