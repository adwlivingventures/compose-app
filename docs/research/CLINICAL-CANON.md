## 1. The Core Model — one loop, two entry points

Performance anxiety is a **self-image problem expressed through the autonomic nervous system**. The clinical and the psychological accounts are the same loop:

1. The man holds a self-image: *"my body fails under pressure."*
2. That self-image runs surveillance — **spectatoring** (Masters & Johnson): he mentally dissociates and watches himself from a critical third-person view, monitoring firmness, elapsed time, anticipated partner disappointment.
3. Surveillance is threat posture → **sympathetic nervous system activation** → cortisol + adrenaline. Adrenaline is a potent **vasoconstrictor**: it drains blood from erectile tissue and suppresses endothelial **nitric oxide** (the mediator of corporal smooth-muscle relaxation). It also puts the ejaculatory reflex on a hair-trigger.
4. The body delivers the predicted failure (psychogenic ED and/or PE). Failure is recorded as evidence. The self-image hardens. Return to 1.

**Why pills alone fail:** PDE5 inhibitors and topical anesthetics address a localized peripheral symptom; they cannot override an active sympathetic fight-or-flight lock or the cognitive spectatoring that drives it. This is the clinical basis for a behavioral, identity-level intervention.

**The intervention thesis:** the 75-Day Protocol breaks the loop at three points — Phase 1 at physiology (step 3), Phase 2 at attention (step 2), Phase 3 at the self-image itself (step 1). Autonomic work and identity work are the *same intervention at two entry points*. The corollary that filters every feature: **COMPOSE never asks a man to perform better; it installs a man for whom there is nothing to perform.** Address the doer, not the doing.

**Prevalence anchors (for copy/positioning, not medical claims):** Sexual performance anxiety affects ~9–25% of men. GSAB: ~50% of sexually active adults report ≥1 sexual problem; <19% seek help. Barriers are stigma and lack of private infrastructure — which is precisely the wedge for a discreet, private digital tool.

## 2. The Four Clinical Modalities (mechanism → product surface)

### 2.1 Pelvic Floor Down-Training (the Reverse Kegel / "Pelvic Drop")
The central and most-misunderstood mechanic. Men with PE/psychogenic ED typically have **hypertonic** (chronically over-tight, over-contracted) pelvic floors — especially the bulbospongiosus and ischiocavernosus. A hypertonic floor restricts blood flow and keeps the ejaculatory reflex in high tension: a neurological hair-trigger. **Standard Kegels (contractions) make this worse** by raising resting tone and sympathetic drive.

COMPOSE uses the **Reverse Kegel** almost exclusively: conscious diaphragmatic breathing synced to intentional *lengthening and relaxation* of the perineum. Mechanism: on a deep inhale the diaphragm lowers, pushing abdominal organs down, which forces the pelvic floor to expand and drop — reducing pressure on the pudendal nerve, maximizing arterial inflow, delaying the ejaculatory reflex, and stimulating the vagus nerve (parasympathetic) via slow exhalation.
- **Product surface:** the Autonomic Pelvic Pacer — animated loop, **4s inhale + drop / 6s exhale + neutral return** (prolonged exhale is the vagal lever). User logs a 1–5 self-rated control score.
- **Evidence:** biofeedback PFMT down-training improves IELT, especially in *acquired* PE. Stop-start + sphincter-control training raises IELT further.
- **Screening is mandatory** (see §3). Prescribing contractions to a hypertonic floor is a clinical error the app must design out.

### 2.2 Cognitive Behavioral Sex Therapy (CBST)
Targets the cognitive distortions that sustain the loop: **catastrophizing** ("one lost erection ends the relationship") and **all-or-nothing thinking** ("it must be flawless and long to count"), plus rigid "macho" performance beliefs. Method: identify trigger → capture the automatic negative thought → replace with a balanced, reality-based reframe. Trials show CBST matches or beats sildenafil on anxiety reduction in younger men with nonorganic ED, and improves ejaculatory control.
- **Product surface:** the **Thought Restructurer** — a deterministic closed-loop wizard. User picks their distortion from a curated list; app returns a pre-authored, versioned clinical reframe to read aloud. **No generative AI, ever** (§7 of CLAUDE.md). The read-aloud step is the cognitive-defusion mechanism (distancing from the thought).

### 2.3 Mindfulness-Based Sex Therapy (MBST)
The direct antidote to spectatoring (evidence base: Dr. Lori Brotto). Trains non-judgmental present-moment awareness of physical sensation, raising **interoceptive awareness** and improving **genital–subjective arousal concordance** (mind and body registering arousal together, without the stress-hormone overlay). Attention is redirected from performance cues to tactile feedback and breath; distracting thoughts are allowed to pass.
- **Product surface:** the daily **Auditory Anchor** (2–4 min neuro-acoustically engineered audio delivering somatic grounding). **Field POV only** — first-person, through his own eyes/skin. Observer-POV imagery ("watch yourself…") is spectatoring training and is banned.

### 2.4 Sensate Focus + Polyvagal Co-Regulation
A graded hierarchy of **non-demand touch** (Masters & Johnson; Weiner & Avery-Clark's 2017 mindfulness adaptation shifts the goal from mutual pleasure to present-moment sensory awareness — texture, temperature, pressure). Removing intercourse and orgasm as goals dissolves anticipatory anxiety and lets the parasympathetic system restore natural arousal. If an erection occurs, let it go — teaching the body arousal is a response, not a test.

Stages: (1) non-genital touch (breasts/genitals excluded, intercourse forbidden); (2) genital/breast touch, still no performance goal, erection → divert to non-genital area; (3) adding lotion/lubricant (new tactile medium; never oil near latex); (4) mutual touching; (5) sensual containment/intercourse — top position for the receiver, no thrusting, containment and shared breath first.
- **Product surface:** partner-facing content is **locked until Day 76** (Mastery Suite / "Sensate Mastery"). Locking it until after the solo somatic reset prevents premature relapse into performance-based spectatoring. Partner participation dramatically improves outcomes (couples-linked digital sex therapy: large gains in satisfaction/connection, reduced distress) — but it is an *earned* Act II unlock, not a launch-scope solo feature.
- **Adherence is the delivery risk:** online sex-therapy adherence often runs <60%; couples rush stages the moment ED improves and re-trigger anxiety. Mitigation: **gatekeeping** — require ≥3 logged Stage-1 sessions over a mandatory ~2-week window, with post-session journaling, before Stage 2 unlocks.

## 3. Diagnostic Screening — hypertonic vs hypotonic (safety-critical)

The onboarding diagnostic must branch **before** prescribing any pelvic work. Two opposite states need opposite therapy:

| State | Drivers | Symptom signature | Intervention |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Hypertonic (tight)** — the default assumption for this population | Chronic stress, clenching, postural load | PE, painful/rapid ejaculation, psychogenic ED, perineal tension, urinary hesitancy, post-void dribble | Reverse Kegels, diaphragmatic breathing, down-training stretches |
| **Hypotonic (weak)** | Sedentary, atrophy, aging, post-surgical | Can't maintain erection, weak/dribbling ejaculation, rapid sensation rise | Standard Kegels (concentric strengthening) |

Branch signals: hypertonic → trouble starting urination, pelvic/testicular tension, post-void dribble; performance-anxiety pattern → intermittent partnered ED with spared morning erections, "rush to finish." **Never instruct mid-stream urine stoppage** (bladder/UTI risk). Supporting down-training stretches: bridges, alternating pelvic tilts, butterfly, happy baby — all breath-paced, glutes/abs kept flaccid.

## 4. The 75-Day Architecture — why 75, not 30

Grounded in habit-automaticity science (Lally / UCL, 2010): automaticity follows an **asymptotic curve** (Mitscherlich's law of diminishing returns), not a fixed "21 days." Average time to plateau is **~66 days**, range **18–254**, and **complex** cognitive+physical routines take ~**1.5×** longer than simple ones. The 75-day window = the 66-day clinical average + a ~13.6% neuroplastic buffer for high complexity.

Neurobiology of the transition: early practice is **prefrontal/hippocampal** — effortful, willpower-dependent. With repetition against a stable cue, control migrates to the **basal ganglia**, which chunks the sequence into one automatic unit; **myelination** speeds the pathway and makes it resistant to conscious override. By Day 75 the goal is an intimate-situation response that runs from grounded basal-ganglia automaticity rather than prefrontal panic.

**Compliance design (critical):** Lally proved a single missed day does **not** disrupt the long-term trajectory (automaticity dips <0.5 on the SRHI sub-scale, recovers next day). Therefore **no streak-reset shame, ever** — frame progress as a cumulative, resilient neural trajectory. A missed day is a skipped vote, not a lost election (Atomic Habits). This is doctrine, not preference.

**Three phases (25 days each):**
- **Phase 1 — Autonomic Reset (1–25):** silence the sympathetic lock; baseline pelvic-drop pacing; break the intimacy→anxiety association. Register: *capability* ("my body works; it's been following a threat signal I'm retraining").
- **Phase 2 — Exposure & Somatic Control (26–50):** build tolerance to high arousal without losing present-moment control; solo pacer play, vascular conditioning, early solo Sensate Focus; cognitive defusion. Register: *evidence* ("the pause is a tool now, not an escape").
- **Phase 3 — Identity Consolidation (51–75):** anchor the shift as self-sustaining; core-belief reframing, self-image consolidation. Register: *identity* ("I am someone who trains composure daily").

## 5. The Daily Loop (deterministic, <10 min, all-or-nothing)

Fixed order — **regulate → embody → evidence** — because identity content only lands on a down-regulated nervous system (state-before-statement, §7):
1. **Auditory Anchor (3–4 min):** MBST cognitive reframe or somatic grounding. Manual audio scrubbers are **removed** — surrendering to the pacing is the point; it forces a parasympathetic rhythm and establishes clinical authority (deliberate friction against the "rush to finish").
2. **Physical Conditioning / Pelvic Pacer (5 min):** breath+pelvic-floor sequence (4s drop / 6s release); log 1–5 control score.
3. **Vitality Checklist (1 min):** three binary check-ins — Presence Work (time spent embodied), Clean Focus (avoided porn), Vitality Habit (protected sleep/morning light).

**Point of No Return (PONR) awareness** and the arousal-plateau concept are trained through the pacer + control score, teaching the man to recognize the pre-ejaculatory clench and counter it with a conscious drop.

**Thought Restructurer** fires on demand when anxiety spikes (see §2.2). **SOS/Triage** is reachable in one tap from anywhere — the Post-Setback Protocol: an audio track that intercepts catastrophizing after a real-world setback (the highest-churn moment), reframes failure as a normal neurobiological fluctuation, and prevents the cortisol/dopamine crash that precedes cancellation.

## 6. Supporting Neurobiology & Reconditioning

**Refractory period (for the Act II "Refractory Window Guide"):** post-ejaculation, **prolactin** surges (elevated ~60 min, suppressing dopaminergic drive), alongside **oxytocin** (satiety/bonding; surge scale tracks refractory duration), **serotonin** (inhibitory), and likely **GnIH**. A peripheral model adds seminal-vesicle wall-tension feedback. Age is the dominant variable (~15 min at 18 vs 12–24h+ past 40). Framing use: the "autonomic acclimation window" — consecutive/second-round encounters carry lower anticipatory adrenaline and can be reframed from "first-round failure" into a retraining window.

**Dopamine resensitization:** high-speed, novelty-heavy porn conditions the brain to sprint to climax on minimal friction. Reconditioning = solo somatic training with zero visual media, edging toward the threshold then dropping arousal via diaphragmatic breath + reverse kegel, repeating — teaching tolerance of high somatic excitement without the ejaculatory cascade. (The Vitality Checklist's "Clean Focus" indexes this.)

**Community-validated strategies (from qualitative forum synthesis — informs relatable copy):** the penetration ban ("outercourse" with intercourse explicitly off the table); decentering the penis (pivot to hands/mouth/toys instead of shutting down); dopamine resensitization; radical partner communication that lowers the "threat level" in the room. Partners are often *more* frustrated by the man's shutdown reaction than the erection loss itself.

**Reject and warn against the "Angion Method"** (viral penile vascular-massage trend): zero peer-reviewed validation; aggressive manual friction on tunica albuginea / sinusoidal tissue risks plaque, scarring, and Peyronie's. COMPOSE positions as the safe, clinically grounded alternative and cautions against traction/force techniques.

## 7. Subconscious Engineering Rules (feature checklist — from Psychological Foundations)

Every feature touching identity/belief must pass all of these:
1. **State before statement** — somatic down-regulation precedes every identity impression; anxious entry routes to a regulating on-ramp or SOS first.
2. **Right register for the day** — capability (P1) → evidence (P2) → identity/"I am" (P3) → custodial (Act II/III). Never use a later-phase register early; earlier registers stay legal later. Onboarding/paywall may name the destination identity in *second* person as a vision, never assert it in first person yet.
3. **Field POV only** in all imagery/rehearsal. No observer POV.
4. **Never impress the negative** — no deficit-restating ("don't panic," "stop worrying"), no negation-framed instruction, no urgency/loss framing. Old scripts appear only externalized and labeled (the `oldScript` pattern). This is the doctrinal root of the notification rules: an urgency push is a negative impression delivered past the critical faculty.
5. **Repetition is scheduled, not browsable** — impression content is day-keyed and session-gated, not shelved in a library where it decays into content.
6. **Feeling-paired** — identity statements ride on breath/audio/settled visual rhythm; audio > text for impression; bare-text affirmation walls banned.
7. **Generation beats consumption** — the man chooses, arranges, or signs (oath, graduation choice, the credo artifact) rather than merely reading. Free text is optional-only and local-only, never telemetered.
8. **Votes, not verdicts** — count evidence of behavior ("Day 40 complete" = a vote for "a man who trains composure daily"); never grade the self with percentages, bars, or pass/fail. The self is never measured; content length may be.
9. **Deterministic, authored, versioned** — all of the above ships as content files. The doctrine makes content *feel* personal; the code stays deterministic.

Self-talk register law (Helmstetter): authored first-person copy must be **Level III ("I no longer") or Level IV ("I am")**. Level II ("I need to," "I should") restates deficit and is banned as first-person. Motivation-source migration (Siebold) is the explicit arc: he arrives running *from* shame (fear/scarcity); by Phase 3 copy must move his stated reason to *toward*-motivation (presence, the man he is). Take Siebold's structure (scheduled emotional re-supply, vision over escape), discard his temperature — hustle/intensity vocabulary (attack, dominate, outwork) is sympathetic-activation language and is banned even where the source endorses it.

## 8. Conversion & Retention Psychology (reconciled with Model V2)

**Why he converts:** the diagnostic onboarding performs the self-image diagnosis he's never had words for — naming the loop (adrenaline trap, spectatoring, novelty loop) *is* the "aha." Recognizing his own unspoken symptoms triggers the sense that the app understands him better than his physician. The invested cognitive/emotional energy of the diagnostic makes paywall abandonment feel like a loss. Commitment precedes change (oath/consistency). The **hard paywall** is doctrine-consistent: a half-sampled identity program is one the old identity gets to audit and reject.

**Why he stays 75 days:** the loop is too small to lose to friction (<10 min), anchored to a stable cue (habit stacking — his choice at onboarding), every completion framed as a vote. Churn concentrates where **evidence lags feeling** (~Days 10–20, "nothing's happening") — which is why the **Day-14 re-measurement** exists: manufactured visible evidence at the exact doubt window ("measured, not promised"), alongside relapse-normalization content.

**Why he stays the year:** graduation converts the program into an identity he *owns* (the Composure Credo artifact; the Day-76 unlock is a ceremony, never a sales moment). Act II/III is custodial-register maintenance + quarterly re-measurement — the same evidence-not-promises engine at lower cadence. The month-11 renewal-evidence screen shows him his own year of data before the charge.

**Legitimate mechanics** (retained): sunk-cost/commitment escalation *through genuine diagnostic investment*; authority via an "analyzer" compile screen that inflates perceived value of a bespoke protocol; the **Zeigarnik effect** on the Day-76 lock (the brain fixates on uncompleted/locked items); a single free "unlocked preview" of a Mastery module to open a curiosity gap. Social proof for shame-normalization must come from **honest aggregate cohort data** (via the consented anonymous telemetry, §7 of CLAUDE.md), framed to remind him he's part of a normalized movement, not a defective outlier.

**Unit-economics context (diligence framing, not launch copy):** health/wellness SaaS median LTV:CAC ~3.4:1; top-quartile (NRR >120%) ~5.6:1; healthy floor 3:1. 12-month wellness-subscription LTV ~$180–260. The Graduation Loop (Act II/III expansion) is the mechanism that pushes NRR toward/past 100% and turns recovered users into retained members — the acquisition-diligence asset the consented telemetry is designed to evidence.

## 9. Regulatory & Positioning Guardrails

Position strictly as a **General Wellness** product — "somatic presence," "nervous system regulation," "intimacy confidence" — under the FDCA §520(o) / 21st Century Cures general-wellness exemption for low-risk products unrelated to diagnosis/cure/treatment. **Never claim to "cure"/"treat" ED or PE** in app, metadata, or marketing; it is an educational/behavioral pacing tool, not a diagnostic device. Physician-screening framing in onboarding stands (organic causes are real and out of scope). Any deterministic "copilot" stays a closed decision tree that provides cognitive *reframes*, not diagnostic/treatment directives — keeping it clear of FDA CDS device criteria (no signal acquisition; no specific directive a user would blindly follow; user can review the basis). Privacy: local-first, discretion/stranger-test on every external surface (CLAUDE.md §6).

**Competitor gaps COMPOSE occupies:** pure pelvic trainers (Dr. Kegel, Stamena) ignore cognition and can worsen hypertonicity; video-course libraries (Mojo, Lover.io) lack daily accountability and induce analysis-paralysis; sexual-meditation apps (Rino) lack somatic metrics and physical tracking; couples apps (Blueheart) require a partner before the man has repaired his solo identity. The open vertical: identity-first neurobiological therapeutic fusing MBST + pelvic down-training metrics + solo-first privacy in a discreet, grounding shell.
