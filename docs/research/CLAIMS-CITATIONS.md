## The Crutch card — "50% of men stop taking ED pills — many even when they work, rather than let intimacy depend on a tablet."

1. **Carvalheira A, Forjaz V, Pereira NM (2012).** "Dropout in the Treatment
   of Erectile Dysfunction with PDE5: A Study on Predictors and a Qualitative
   Analysis of Reasons for Discontinuation." *Journal of Sexual Medicine*,
   9(9). Finding: **48.9% (160/327) discontinued** PDE5i treatment; authors'
   conclusion: "half the subjects discontinued medication." Reasons:
   non-effectiveness 36.8%, **psychological factors 17.5%** (anxiety, negative
   emotions, fears, dysfunctional beliefs), erection recovery 14.4%,
   cardiovascular concerns 8.7%.
   Record: https://research.ulusofona.pt/pt/publications/dropout-in-the-treatment-of-erectile-dysfunction-with-pde5-a-stud/

2. **Kim S-C, Lee Y-S, Seo K-K, Jung G-W, Kim T-H (2014).** "Reasons and
   predictive factors for discontinuation of PDE-5 inhibitors despite
   successful intercourse in erectile dysfunction patients." *International
   Journal of Impotence Research*, 26:87–93. Finding: **23.9% (116/485)
   discontinued despite successful intercourse**; #1 reason (31.0%):
   **reluctance toward medication-dependent intercourse**; spontaneous
   recovery 30.2%; cost 26.7%.
   Record: https://www.nature.com/articles/ijir201341

Claim mapping: "50% stop" ← study 1 ("about half"). "Many even when they
work, rather than let intimacy depend on a tablet" ← study 2 (23.9% despite
success; top reason medication-dependence reluctance). No other quantitative
