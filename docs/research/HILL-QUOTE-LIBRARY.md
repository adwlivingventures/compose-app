None of those lines appear in Hill's books. That script is **copyrighted by the channel** — no line from it may ship in COMPOSE, in ASO text, or in marketing. It is used here only as a thematic map.
2. **The 1937 first-edition text of *Think and Grow Rich* is public domain in the US.** Every quote below is drawn from that text. These may be reproduced, adapted, and excerpted freely, with attribution ("Napoleon Hill, *Think and Grow Rich*, 1937") — which is also stronger provenance on a paywall or Anchor intro than an anonymous YouTube channel.
3. **Later editions are NOT public domain.** Quote only 1937 wording. If a quote's wording can't be verified against the 1937 text, it doesn't ship.

5. **Domain adaptation flag.** Quotes marked ⚠ reference money/riches/poverty literally. Hill's mechanism transfers cleanly (riches → composure/presence), but the wording must be adapted or the quote introduced with framing ("Hill wrote about wealth; the mechanism is identical for composure"). ## 3. Why placement matters (product psychology layer)

**Tag legend** — Register: `GROUNDING` (down-regulating, agency-restoring) · `INSTRUCTIVE` (mechanism-teaching) · `CONFRONTING` (challenge, manageable activation) · `AFFIRMING` (identity-consolidating, first-person). Surface: `AA` Auditory Anchor script seed · `TR` Thought Restructurer reframe seed · `PS` phase-script epigraph · `MS` mirror-sentence / oath seed (Newsreader italic surface).

The placement logic is polyvagal, not editorial. A Phase 1 user arrives sympathetically activated; a CONFRONTING quote there reads as one more demand from a world already scoring him — it confirms the performance frame instead of dissolving it. So Phase 1 gets only GROUNDING/INSTRUCTIVE material whose common move is *agency without demand*: "this state is controllable" rather than "control it." Phase 2 is exposure work; CONFRONTING quotes are deliberately introduced *there* because manageable cognitive challenge during rising arousal tolerance is exposure-congruent — the quote itself is a small stressor he metabolizes. Phase 3 is self-schema work: AFFIRMING first-person material only, because by Day 51 the job is consistency pressure ("this is who I am now"), and first-person present-tense language is what self-perception theory says consolidates it.

## 8. EXCLUDED — and why (this section is the tone spec, negatively defined)

- **"He left himself no possible way of retreat. He had to win or perish!" / "burn his ships and cut all sources of retreat." (Ch. 2)** — Maximum-stakes framing is sympathetic ignition. - **"POVERTY CONSCIOUSNESS WILL VOLUNTARILY SEIZE THE MIND…" (Ch. 9) and all literal money/riches lines (⚠ set)** — Off-domain vocabulary; the mechanism quotes above carry the same content without adaptation debt. Full ⚠ list retained in Appendix B for reference.
- **"A busy person seldom has time to think about dying." (Ch. 15)** — Avoidance framed as remedy; contradicts exposure logic.
- **Ether/vibration/broadcasting passages (Ch. 13, parts of Ch. 3)** — 1937 physics-mysticism. The brain-as-radio frame would cost clinical credibility; everything it does is done better by the dominating-thoughts line (P3-07).
- **"Men who accumulate great riches always protect themselves against this evil! The poverty stricken never do!" (Ch. 15)** — Contempt-adjacent class framing; violates the non-judgmental tone rule outright.

## 9. Appendix A — Self-Confidence Formula, verbatim 1937 wording

> "First. I know that I have the ability to achieve the object of my Definite Purpose in life, therefore, I DEMAND of myself persistent, continuous action toward its attainment, and I here and now promise to render such action.
> Second. I realize the dominating thoughts of my mind will eventually reproduce themselves in outward, physical action, and gradually transform themselves into physical reality, therefore, I will concentrate my thoughts for thirty minutes daily, upon the task of thinking of the person I intend to become, thereby creating in my mind a clear mental picture of that person.
> Third. I know through the principle of auto-suggestion, any desire that I persistently hold in my mind will eventually seek expression through some practical means of attaining the object back of it, therefore, I will devote ten minutes daily to demanding of myself the development of SELF-CONFIDENCE.
> Fourth. I have clearly written down a description of my DEFINITE CHIEF AIM in life, and I will never stop trying, until I shall have developed sufficient self-confidence for its attainment.
> Fifth. I fully realize that no wealth or position can long endure, unless built upon truth and justice, therefore, I will engage in no transaction which does not benefit all whom it affects. I will succeed by attracting to myself the forces I wish to use, and the cooperation of other people. I will induce others to serve me, because of my willingness to serve others. I will eliminate hatred, envy, jealousy, selfishness, and cynicism, by developing love for all humanity, because I know that a negative attitude toward others can never bring me success. I will cause others to believe in me, because I will believe in them, and in myself. I will sign my name to this formula, commit it to memory, and repeat it aloud once a day, with full FAITH that it will gradually influence my THOUGHTS and ACTIONS so that I will become a self-reliant, and successful person."
