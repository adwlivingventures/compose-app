# COMPOSE — Psychological Foundations & Identity-Transformation Doctrine

**Purpose.** This file is the psychological source doctrine for COMPOSE. It ports the
identity-transformation corpus proven in the founder's prior work (Psycho-Cybernetics,
*What to Say When You Talk to Yourself*, *The Power of Your Subconscious Mind*, *Atomic
Habits*, *177 Mental Toughness Secrets of the World Class*, and the founder's Morning
Formula identity system) into COMPOSE-specific engineering and copy rules. Every future
feature, script, notification, and paywall line should be checkable against this file.

**Precedence.** CLAUDE.md governs. Nothing here overrides the deterministic-content
constraint (§7), the claims guardrail (§1), the discretion rules (§6), Model V2 (§2), or
the Anti-Roadmap (PRODUCT-VISION.md §3). This file deepens the *why* and supplies the
doctrine those constraints protect.

## 1. The Unifying Thesis

Performance anxiety is a **self-image problem expressed through the autonomic nervous
system**. These are not two explanations — they are one loop:

1. The man carries a self-image: *"I am a man whose body fails under pressure."*
2. The self-image acts as a servo-mechanism (Maltz): it steers behavior, attention, and
   physiology toward confirmation of itself. Spectatoring is not a quirk — it is the
   self-image running surveillance, gathering evidence for the verdict it already holds.
3. Surveillance is a threat posture. Threat posture is sympathetic activation. Adrenaline
   is vasoconstrictive and pro-ejaculatory. The body delivers exactly what the self-image
   predicted.
4. The failure is recorded as evidence. The self-image hardens. Return to 1.

**Therefore:** the 75-Day Protocol is not "anxiety management with identity flavoring."
The autonomic work (breath, pelvic down-training, exposure) and the identity work
(rewires, oaths, anchors) are the *same intervention at two entry points of one loop*.
Phase 1 breaks the loop at step 3 (physiology), Phase 2 at step 2 (attention), Phase 3 at
step 1 (the self-image itself). Any feature that serves only one entry point must at
minimum not damage the other.

**The corollary that filters everything:** COMPOSE never asks a man to *perform better*.
It installs a man for whom there is nothing to perform. Willpower features train the old
identity to try harder; identity features make the old identity obsolete. When evaluating
any feature or copy, ask: *does this address the doer, or the doing?* COMPOSE addresses
the doer.

## 2. The Five-Source Doctrine

### 2.1 Psycho-Cybernetics (Maltz) — the self-image as servo-mechanism

**Core mechanism.** A person cannot consistently outperform their self-image; the nervous
system is a goal-striving mechanism that hits the target the self-image sets, including
negative targets. Crucially, the nervous system **cannot distinguish a vividly imagined
experience from a real one** — synthetic experience deposits the same evidence as lived
experience.

**COMPOSE translations (binding):**
- Spectatoring is named in-app as the self-image's surveillance system, not a bad habit.
  Copy may say "the watcher," "the evaluator," "the commentary" — externalized, never
  fused with the man himself.
- **Synthetic experience is a legitimate content category.** Guided first-person imagery
  of calm, present encounters deposits success-evidence the self-image must account for.
  (See FEATURE-BUILD-PROMPTS-IDENTITY.md, The Rehearsal Room.)
- **Field POV rule (hard):** all imagery and rehearsal content is written through the
  man's own eyes and skin — interoceptive, first-person. Observer-POV imagery ("see
  yourself in the room…") is *spectatoring training* and is banned.
- The "dartboard rule": never set the self-image a target it can miss tonight. Targets
  are always process-side (breath, attention, contact), never outcome-side (rigidity,
  duration). This is the doctrinal root of the Anti-Roadmap's no-output-tracking rule.

### 2.2 What to Say When You Talk to Yourself (Helmstetter) — the self-talk register

**Core mechanism.** The subconscious executes whatever program is repeated to it most,
regardless of truth or intent. Self-talk has levels: Level I (negative acceptance — "I
can't"), Level II (recognition, no resolution — "I need to," "I should"), Level III
(decision to change — "I no longer"), Level IV (the new self — "I am"), Level V
(universal affirmation — unused here).

**COMPOSE translations (binding):**
- **Every first-person sentence the app puts in the man's mouth or mind must be Level
  III or Level IV.** No exceptions. "I need to relax" is Level II and banned as authored
  first-person copy — it repeats the deficit. The Rewires already model this correctly:
  old script externalized and labeled, replacement at Level III/IV.
- Level I material may appear **only** as externalized, quoted, labeled content (the
  `oldScript` pattern) — printed, named for what it is, never voiced as the app's own
  register.
- The app's second-person voice (instructions, tonight's lines) follows the same
  gradient: no deficit-restating ("don't panic," "stop worrying" — both rehearse the
  target word). Direct attention *toward* the replacement behavior, never away from the
  problem.
- Repetition is the delivery mechanism, not a nice-to-have. A Level IV sentence read once
  is a slogan; read daily for 75 days at regulated moments, it is a program. This is why
  the daily loop is all-or-nothing and why identity content is *scheduled* (Rewires,
  anchors) rather than browsable.

### 2.3 The Power of Your Subconscious Mind (Murphy) — the impression mechanics

**Core mechanism.** The subconscious is impressed by (a) repetition, (b) feeling-state at
the moment of impression, and (c) the hypnoidal windows at sleep onset and waking, when
the critical/analytical faculty is quietest. It does not process negation and does not
argue — it accepts what is impressed with feeling and returns it as behavior and
physiology.

**COMPOSE translations (binding):**
- **State-before-statement rule:** identity content lands only on a down-regulated
  nervous system. Every identity statement in the product must be *preceded* by somatic
  settling (breath, body scan, the conditioning orb). An anxious man reading "I am
  composed" is impressing the anxiety, not the sentence. This is why the daily session
  order is fixed: anchor (regulate) → conditioning (embody) → checklist (evidence).
- **The sleep-onset window is the highest-leverage unused surface.** The last rehearsal
  of the day wins the night's consolidation. Today, that rehearsal happens at 3am,
  unsupervised, and it rehearses failure (see CONTENT-STRATEGY.md, "The 3am Rehearsal").
  COMPOSE should own the final impression of the day. (See Nightfall feature spec.)
- **Never impress the negative.** This is the doctrinal root of the notification rules:
  an urgency push ("Don't lose your streak") is a *negative impression delivered at
  random moments straight past the critical faculty*. It is not just off-brand — it is
  the product working against its own mechanism.
- Feeling-pairing: identity statements are always coupled to a felt body state (warmth,
  weight, breath), never delivered as bare text when avoidable. Audio > text for
  impression content; text is for comprehension content.

### 2.4 Atomic Habits (Clear) — votes, evidence, and identity-based habits

**Core mechanism.** Lasting change runs identity → process → outcome, not the reverse.
Every action is a vote for a type of person; the self-image updates on *evidence of
behavior*, not on intention. Habits bind to context and cue, and the habit is the
identity under construction, not the result it produces.

**COMPOSE translations (binding):**
- The unit of progress in all copy is **the vote, not the result**: "Day 40 complete" is
  a vote for "a man who trains composure daily." The renewal-evidence screen and Day-14
  re-measurement already run on this doctrine — evidence shown, never promised.
- **Identity votes must be countable but never scoreboard-ed.** Completed days, sessions,
  and re-measurements are evidence; percentage bars, grades, and pass/fail are judgment.
  (Anti-Roadmap alignment: lesson-length bars measure content; the self is never
  measured.)
- Missed days are handled by the two-day rule reframed for this register: a missed day is
  a skipped vote, not a lost election. No resets, no broken-streak vocabulary anywhere.
  Recovery copy points at the next vote, never at the gap.
- Habit stacking is the retention spine: the daily session anchors to an existing stable
  cue (his choice at onboarding — after coffee, after shower). Nightfall anchors to an
  unavoidable one (getting into bed). Features that float free of a cue will churn.

### 2.5 177 Mental Toughness Secrets (Siebold) — motivation source and the coach's frame

**Core mechanism.** The world class operate from love and abundance; the masses operate
from fear and scarcity. Amateurs are motivated by escaping pain (which expires the moment
pain eases); professionals by a compelling vision of who they are becoming. Emotional
motivation must be re-supplied on a schedule — logic doesn't move behavior.

**COMPOSE translations (binding):**
- **Motivation-source migration is the explicit 75-day arc.** The man arrives running
  from shame (fear/scarcity). Phase 1 meets him there without feeding it; by Phase 3 the
  product must have moved his stated reason to *toward*-motivation (presence with his
  partner, the man he is). Copy audits should check: which phase is this screen in, and
  which motivation source does it address?
- The coach's frame is **calm authority, zero cheerleading**: world-class depth, stated
  plainly. No hype, no "you've got this," no exclamation points. The existing tonight's
  lines are the canonical register.
- **The hustle filter stands absolute.** Siebold's intensity vocabulary (attack, dominate,
  outwork) is sympathetic-activation language and is banned even though the source
  endorses it. COMPOSE takes Siebold's *structure* (scheduled emotional re-supply, vision
  over escape) and discards his *temperature*. The anti-75-Hard positioning is doctrine.

### 2.6 The Morning Formula (founder's identity system) — the personal-artifact pattern

The founder's own practice demonstrates the end state COMPOSE installs: a written
identity document (credo, vision, affirmations) read on a schedule, in a regulated state,
revised as evidence accumulates. The product-shaped translation: **the man should leave
Day 75 owning a personal identity artifact he helped assemble, not a completed course.**
Courses end; artifacts are kept. (See The Composure Credo feature spec.) The artifact is
also the Act II/III retention spine — maintenance mode has something *of his* to return
to, and the month-11 renewal-evidence screen has something of his to show.

## 3. The Identity Register Arc (copy law, by phase)

Identity language is earned, not front-loaded. A Day-3 user told "you are a composed man"
experiences dissonance (the subconscious rejects what contradicts current evidence — the
statement bounces and the miss is recorded). The register escalates only as evidence
accumulates:

| Phase | Days | Register | First-person shape | Example (canonical) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 — Autonomic Reset | 1–25 | **Capability** | "My body knows how / I am learning" | "My body works. It has been following a threat signal — and I am retraining the signal." |
| 2 — Exposure & Mastery | 26–50 | **Evidence** | "I have / I no longer" (Level III) | "Twenty-six days of reps are in the system. The pause is a tool now, not an escape." |
| 3 — Identity Consolidation | 51–75 | **Identity** | "I am" (Level IV) | "I am someone who trains composure daily. The struggle was a pattern, not a person." |
| Act II/III | 76–365 | **Custodial** | "I remain / I keep" | "I keep what I built. Maintenance is who I am now, not a chore." |

Rules: never use a register from a later phase on an earlier-phase surface; earlier
registers remain legal later (a Phase 3 screen may use capability language). Onboarding
and the paywall live *before* Phase 1: they may name the destination identity in second
person as a vision ("the man you're becoming"), but never assert it in first person yet.

## 4. Subconscious Engineering Rules (feature-level checklist)

Any new feature touching identity or belief content must satisfy all of these:

1. **State before statement.** Somatic down-regulation precedes every identity
   impression. If the feature can be entered while acutely anxious, it must begin with a
   regulating on-ramp or route to SOS first.
2. **Right register for the day.** Content is keyed to protocol day and uses §3's table.
3. **Field POV only** in all imagery/rehearsal content. No observer POV, ever.
4. **Never impress the negative.** No deficit-restating, no negation-framed instructions,
   no urgency. Old scripts appear only externalized and labeled.
5. **Repetition is scheduled, not browsable.** Impression content is served (day-keyed,
   session-gated), not shelved in a library where it decays into content. Comprehension
   content (mechanism education) belongs in the Library iceberg.
6. **Feeling-paired.** Identity statements ride on breath pacing, audio, or a settled
   visual rhythm. Bare-text affirmation walls are banned.
7. **Generation beats consumption.** Where possible the man chooses, arranges, or signs —
   he does not merely read (generation effect; the oath and the graduation choice are the
   pattern). Free text is optional-only and local-only, never telemetered (§7).
8. **Votes, not verdicts.** Anything counted is evidence of behavior; nothing displayed
   grades the self. When in doubt, consult the Anti-Roadmap — the Observer Effect rule is
   the sharpest edge here.
9. **Deterministic, authored, versioned.** All of the above ships as content files (§7).
   The doctrine makes the content *feel* personal; the code stays deterministic.

## 5. Conversion & Retention Psychology (where doctrine meets Model V2)

- **Why he converts:** the diagnostic onboarding converts because it performs the
  self-image diagnosis the man has never had words for — naming the loop (adrenaline
  trap, spectatoring, novelty loop) *is* the "aha," and the paywall sells the exit from
  the loop, not app features. The hard paywall is doctrine-consistent: an identity
  program half-sampled is a program the old identity gets to audit and reject. Commitment
  precedes change (oath mechanics, consistency principle).
- **Why he stays 75 days:** the daily loop is small enough to never lose to friction
  (<10 min), scheduled against a stable cue, and every completion is framed as a vote.
  Churn risk concentrates where evidence lags feeling (Days ~10–20, "nothing is
  happening") — which is why the Day-14 re-measurement exists: manufactured visible
  evidence at the exact doubt window, and why relapse-normalization content (Success
  Vault, rewires on falter days) sits in the same window.
- **Why he stays the year:** graduation converts the program into an identity he owns
  (credo artifact, unlock ceremony that is never a sales moment). Act II/III retention is
  custodial-register maintenance plus quarterly re-measurement — the same
  evidence-not-promises engine at lower cadence. The month-11 renewal notice showing his
  own year of data is the doctrine's final form: the product never argues; it shows him
  who he has become and lets consistency do the rest.
