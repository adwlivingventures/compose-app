Welcome to Day 31. Let your shoulders fall, and breathe deep. There is a specific feeling that accompanies performance anxiety: Urgency. A frantic, internal voice screaming, 'Hurry up before you lose it!' or 'Go faster so you can finish before you go soft!'

This urgency is an illusion. It is a symptom of adrenaline, not a physical requirement. When you feel that frantic urge to speed up today, do the exact opposite. Move in slow motion. Slow your hands. Slow your breath. Deliberately moving slowly sends a powerful biofeedback signal to your brain that there is no emergency. You kill the urgency by refusing to obey it.
