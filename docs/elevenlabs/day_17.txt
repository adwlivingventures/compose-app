Welcome to Day 17... <break time="1.5s" /> Drop your shoulders, and breathe into your stomach. Let's talk about the moment things soften. For an anxious man, losing rigidity mid-encounter feels like falling off a cliff. Panic sets in, the jaw clenches, and the mental script screams, 'It’s happening again.'

We are going to reframe this biologically. When you soften, your body is simply pulling back to breathe. It is a Soft Reset. When this happens, your only job is to stay physically close to your partner and keep breathing. Do not pull away. Do not apologize profusely. Stroke her hair, kiss her neck, and let your body rest at a level two or three for a few minutes. When you remove the panic from a Soft Reset, the blood flow naturally returns. The only thing keeping it away is your fear.
