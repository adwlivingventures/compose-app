Welcome to Day 46. Let your stomach expand... and breathe out. Despite all your training, there will be encounters where the spectator wins. You will falter.

The success of your protocol is not determined by whether you falter; it is determined by how you pivot. When it happens, do not roll over in shame. Pivot immediately back to Sensate Focus. Say, 'My system just spiked. Let me focus on you for a while.' Switch to your hands. Switch to your warmth. When you prove to your brain that a falter doesn't end the intimacy, the brain stops fearing the falter.
