Welcome to Day 65... <break time="1.5s" /> Settle in. If you find yourself in a state of acute hyper-arousal where the 5-and-Drop isn't enough, we use the 4-7-8 acoustic override—the same protocol behind the Steady Me tool you carry in this app.

Inhale quietly through your nose for four seconds. Hold the breath—without clenching your chest—for seven seconds. Then, exhale forcefully through your mouth, making a 'whoosh' sound, for eight seconds. The combination of the extended hold, the long exhale, and the acoustic vibration of the 'whoosh' forces a massive parasympathetic reset. It is a biological circuit breaker. Use it when the fire gets too hot.
