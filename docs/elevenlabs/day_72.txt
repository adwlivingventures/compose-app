Welcome to Day 72. Settle in... drop your shoulders. Let us revisit the paradox of control. The men who try the hardest to grip, force, and mentally command their bodies to perform are the ones who suffer the most dysfunction.

True autonomic control is not an iron grip. It is an open hand. It is the deep, biological confidence that allows you to let go, knowing you have the tools to catch yourself if you need to. Stop gripping the wheel. Surrender to the connection.
