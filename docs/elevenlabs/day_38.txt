Welcome to Day 38. Take a slow breath... and let it go. When performance anxiety hits, men often close their eyes. We retreat into the darkness of our own minds, trying to focus on a fantasy or force an outcome.

Closing your eyes feeds the spectator. Today, use the Visual Anchor. Keep your eyes open. Look at your partner. Look at her skin, her eyes, her expressions. When you force your brain to process live, visual data of the human being in front of you, it cannot simultaneously run a background simulation of your anxieties. Eye contact pulls you out of your head and into the room.
