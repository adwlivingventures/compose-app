There is a powerful cultural myth that a healthy male body operates like a light switch—that once you are physically aroused, you must remain at one hundred percent rigidity until the moment of release. This is biologically false.

Intimacy has natural waves. Blood flow ebbs, flows, softens, and firms based on changes in touch, position, and focus. But right now, if your body softens for even five seconds, your brain catastrophizes. It panics. That panic floods your system with vasoconstricting hormones, turning a natural biological fluctuation into a permanent adrenaline lock.

Today, we neutralize the metric. If your body softens, it is not a catastrophe, and it does not mean you are broken. It means your body is breathing. Allow the wave. Do not fight it. When you remove the panic of losing an erection, you remove the very anxiety that causes you to lose it.
