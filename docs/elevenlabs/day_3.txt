Welcome to Day 3... <break time="1.5s" /> Today, we connect the mind to your pelvic muscles. When your nervous system is anxious, your body unconsciously clenches. You might feel it in your jaw, your neck, or your shoulders. But most crucially, you clench your pelvic floor—the sling of muscles between your pubic bone and tailbone. This chronic tightness, or hypertonicity, acts as a physical hair-trigger for your ejaculatory reflex.

Today, we practice the Pelvic Drop. Take a deep, slow inhale down into your stomach, letting your abdomen expand. As you inhale, imagine gently sending that air all the way down into your pelvis, softening and dropping the muscles between your legs. It should feel like a gentle downward release—widening the space between your sit bones. This drop is the physical brake pedal for your nervous system. Repeat this drop three times today at your desk or in your car. Inhale, expand, and drop.
