Welcome to Day 23. Let's look at how you define victory. Right now, your brain likely categorizes intimacy in black and white: penetration leading to climax is a success, and anything else is a failure.

This binary thinking is a massive source of anxiety. True intimacy is a spectrum. A session where you simply map out touch, practice the pelvic drop, and maintain a calm connection without any penetration is a massive clinical success.

Stop measuring your encounters by the final destination. Measure them by your ability to stay present, relaxed, and connected. When you broaden the definition of success, you systematically dismantle the fear of failure.
