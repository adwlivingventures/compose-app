Welcome to Day 20... <break time="1.5s" /> Men are highly visual creatures. Over years of solitary habits, we train our brains to require extreme visual stimulation or intense mental fantasy to achieve arousal. But when you are with a real partner, relying on a mental movie pulls you entirely out of your body.

You cannot be present with a woman if you are watching a movie in your head. Today, we practice somatic arousal. Close your eyes. Drop the fantasy. Focus entirely on the friction, the warmth, and the rhythm of the actual physical moment. If your mind wanders to an image, gently pull it back to a physical sensation. Train your body to respond to reality, not a projection.
