Welcome to Day 56. When adrenaline spikes during intimacy, blood flow literally leaves your prefrontal cortex—your logic center—and floods your amygdala, the fear center. This is why you feel like you 'lose your mind' or 'black out' right before an early release.

Today, we practice the Prefrontal Override. If you feel the rush of panic, open your eyes. Speak a full sentence out loud to your partner. Force yourself to engage in complex sensory observation. When you speak, or when you consciously analyze the color of her eyes, you physically force blood flow back into the prefrontal cortex, immediately dampening the amygdala's panic signal. Stay online.
