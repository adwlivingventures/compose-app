There is a toxic script we are taught by movies and media: that successful intimacy is always sudden, fiery, and completely spontaneous. Because of this myth, when you feel the need to pause, breathe, or consciously relax your pelvic floor, you feel like you are ruining the mood.

Let’s correct this right now. Intentionality is not a buzzkill. Professional athletes do not sprint onto the field without warming up their nervous systems, yet we expect our bodies to go from zero to one hundred with no preparation. Taking two minutes before intimacy to breathe, drop your pelvic tension, and ground yourself in the room is not a weakness. It is elite physical management. Give yourself permission to prepare. You do not have to be spontaneous to be passionate.
