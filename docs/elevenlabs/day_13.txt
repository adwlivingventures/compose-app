Welcome to Day 13. When your mind races, your breath shortens. It moves up into your chest, becoming shallow and rapid. This chest-breathing signals to your brain that you are under threat, triggering adrenaline and pelvic floor clenching. Your breath is your anchor. It is the only autonomic function in your body that you can consciously control. By forcing your breath down into your diaphragm, expanding your stomach, and slowing your exhale, you physically force your heart rate to drop. You cannot have a panic attack while taking slow, deep diaphragmatic breaths. Let the breath be your master switch.
