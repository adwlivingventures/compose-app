Welcome to Day 47. If you are engaging in solo practice, you must be ruthlessly disciplined about your environment.

If your solo habit involves a dark room, a glowing screen, a clenched jaw, and a stopwatch mentality, you are actively undoing your clinical progress. Your solo practice must mirror your partner goals. Lights on. Eyes open. Slow breath. Deep pelvic drops. If you cannot maintain a relaxed, parasympathetic state during solo touch, you will never maintain it with a partner. Align your training.
