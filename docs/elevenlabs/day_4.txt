Welcome to Day 4... <break time="1.5s" /> Today, we address the clock. Society conditions men to believe that the ultimate measure of their intimate value is the stopwatch—how many minutes they can endure before climax. This turning of the bedroom into a timed performance is what triggers your performance anxiety. Physiological latency is a natural byproduct of a relaxed, parasympathetic state—not a product of mental willpower. When you try to 'force' yourself to last longer, your autonomic system interprets that force as stress, clenches your pelvic muscles, and actually accelerates you toward the finish line.

Your value as a partner is anchored in your presence, your touch, and your connection—not a countdown timer. Today, give yourself permission to drop the clock. Intimacy is a landscape to be felt, not a race to be run.
