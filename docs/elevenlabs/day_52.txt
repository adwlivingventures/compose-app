Welcome to Day 52... <break time="1.5s" /> Let's talk about the physical structure of your brain. When you practice the Pelvic Drop instead of clenching, or when you choose to breathe through an adrenaline wash instead of panicking, you are firing new neural circuits.

In neurobiology, neurons that fire together, wire together. Every time you successfully pause an encounter to regulate your arousal, your brain wraps that new neural pathway in a fatty substance called myelin. Myelin acts like insulation on a copper wire, making the new signal travel faster and stronger. You are literally building a faster, calmer neurological highway. The calm is no longer just a technique. It is becoming your anatomy.
