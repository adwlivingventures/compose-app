Welcome to Day 10. Today we map your somatic pacing. We do not use stopwatches; we use the Arousal Scale. Imagine your physical excitement on a scale from 1, which is completely neutral, to 10, which is the Point of No Return. Anxious men often jump from a level 3 directly to a 9 in seconds because their pelvic floor is clenched tight. Your objective in the coming weeks is to learn the landscape of the 6 and the 7. During solo practice or partner touch, identify your exact physical markers at a level 7. When you hit an 8, use your breath and a deep pelvic drop to bring it back to a 6. Mastery is learning to ride the plateau.
