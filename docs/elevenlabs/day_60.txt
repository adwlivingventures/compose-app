Welcome to Day 60... <break time="1.5s" /> Your nervous system does not exist in isolation. Through a process called limbic resonance, your autonomic state is constantly communicating with your partner's autonomic state. If you are frantic and clenched, she will unconsciously tense up.

But this works in reverse. Today, focus on her breath. Feel the slow, rhythmic rising and falling of her chest. Consciously synchronize your breathing to hers. When you anchor your breath to a calm partner, you utilize her parasympathetic stability to pull your own nervous system out of the sympathetic fire. Become one integrated system.
