Welcome to Day 62... <break time="1.5s" /> The core tenet of Somatic Experiencing is that suffering equals pain multiplied by resistance. When you feel a surge of nervous adrenaline, the resistance—the panicking about the panic—is what locks the tension into your pelvic floor.

Today, practice radical somatic acceptance. If your heart races, let it race. If your body softens, let it soften. Drop your resistance to what your body is doing in the present moment. When you stop fighting the sensation, the sensation loses its power over you. Accept the wave, and it will pass.
