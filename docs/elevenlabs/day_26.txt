Welcome to Day 26, and welcome to Phase 2. Over the last twenty-five days, you learned how to drop your pelvic floor, slow your breath, and quiet the spectator. You built a safe harbor. But true confidence is not about staying in the harbor... <break time="2.0s" /> it is about learning to navigate the storm.

In clinical psychology, we use a tool called Exposure Therapy. You cannot conquer the anxiety of physical arousal by avoiding it. Starting today, we are going to intentionally introduce physical excitement. We are going to turn up the heat, let you feel the adrenaline, and then practice using your Phase 1 tools to bring your system back to baseline.

One note before we begin. Some of the exercises in this phase involve a partner. If you are single right now, do not skip those days. Run the solo version of the pacing work, and treat the partner instructions as rehearsal—you are training the same nervous system either way. The opportunity will come, and your baseline will be ready.

You are no longer running from the intensity. You are stepping into it, knowing you hold the master switch.
