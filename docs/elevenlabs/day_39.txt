Welcome to Day 39... <break time="1.5s" /> As we increase the physical exposure, you will experience natural fluctuations. You will surge to an 8, drop to a 4, firm up, and soften.

I want you to stop merely tolerating these fluctuations, and start actively welcoming them. When your body softens slightly, smile to yourself. It means your Phase 1 training is working. It means your parasympathetic system is coming online and allowing your body to breathe. Do not brace against the ebb. Ride it. The harder you grip the sand, the faster it slips through your fingers.
