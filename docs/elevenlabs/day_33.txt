Welcome to Day 33. Breathe in... <break time="2.0s" /> expand the stomach... <break time="2.0s" /> and let it go. Once you have mastered stillness, we introduce motion using the 10-Second Rule.

When you begin to move, do so for only ten seconds. Then, stop completely. Breathe. Assess your arousal scale. Drop the tension. Then, resume for another ten seconds.

The anxious brain wants a continuous, unbroken sprint to the finish. By breaking the encounter into ten-second micro-sessions, you prevent the momentum from ever leaving your control. You are not running a marathon; you are taking a series of highly controlled, deliberate steps.
