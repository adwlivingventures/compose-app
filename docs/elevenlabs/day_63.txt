Welcome to Day 63. Performance anxiety is rooted in scarcity. We rush because we subconsciously believe the pleasure, or the erection, is a scarce resource that will vanish at any second.

You must cultivate an abundance mindset. You have hours. You have tomorrow. You have next week. When you truly believe that intimacy is abundant, there is absolutely no reason to sprint. Slow your hands. Slow your breath. Drop your pelvis. Operate from a place of total, unhurried abundance.
