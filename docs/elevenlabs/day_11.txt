Welcome to Day 11. One of the most destructive cognitive errors we make in our relationships is Mind Reading. It is the absolute certainty that you know exactly what your partner is thinking—and that she is judging you, disappointed in you, or tensing with frustration. You cannot read minds. You are projecting your own internal performance anxiety onto your partner. Most partners are far more attuned to your emotional presence, your warmth, and your desire for them than the stopwatch metrics of your erection. When you assume she is judging you, you pull away, creating the very distance you fear. Stay in your body, and trust the connection.
