If you struggle with early release, you have likely noticed a frustrating pattern: the first encounter after a multi-day break is almost impossible to control, but if you attempt a second round shortly after, or try again the next day, your control is vastly superior.

This is not a fluke. It is the Autonomic Window. Following ejaculation, your brain undergoes a neurochemical shift, releasing prolactin which naturally dampens the physical sensitivity of your genital nerves. Furthermore, the initial exposure has successfully burned off your baseline performance adrenaline.

Do not view an early release as a failure. View it as clearing the adrenaline. The consecutive encounter is your somatic training ground. It is where your nervous system is calm enough to learn what true control actually feels like.
