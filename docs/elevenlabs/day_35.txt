Welcome to Day 35... <break time="1.5s" /> Let's optimize your breath. When you inhale, your heart rate naturally speeds up slightly. When you exhale, your heart rate slows down.

During moments of high intensity or panic, men tend to gasp—taking sharp, shallow inhales and barely exhaling at all. This locks the body into sympathetic overdrive. Today, focus entirely on the exhale. Make your exhale longer than your inhale—the same rhythm your daily pacer has been training. Breathe in for four seconds... <break time="1.5s" /> and slowly, gently blow the air out for six seconds. A long exhale is the biological off-switch for an adrenaline spike.
