Welcome to Day 42... <break time="1.5s" /> You do not have to manage your anxiety in secret. The burden of carrying it alone is what makes it so heavy.

Today, practice the Partner Check-In. In the middle of an encounter, simply stop, look at your partner, and tell her exactly where your body is at. 'I'm at an 8 right now, let's just breathe for a second.' By speaking it out loud, you release the pressure valve. You bring her into your process as an ally, rather than viewing her as an evaluator.

If you are training solo, speak the check-in out loud anyway. 'I'm at an 8. Breathe.' Hearing your own voice name the number is what interrupts the autopilot. Vulnerability destroys performance anxiety.
