Welcome to Day 37. We need to dismantle the Sunk Cost Fallacy of the bedroom. This is the belief that if you have initiated intimacy, you are absolutely obligated to cross the finish line—no matter how anxious or disconnected you feel.

This obligation is a prison. If you are in the middle of an encounter and your anxiety is peaking, your body softens, or you simply feel disconnected... you are allowed to stop. You can say, 'I love being close to you, but my body needs to rest tonight.' Ending a session without a climax is not a failure. Forcing yourself through a panic attack is. Reclaim your right to stop.
