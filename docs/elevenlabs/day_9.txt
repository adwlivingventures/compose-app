Welcome to Day 9. Take a slow, diaphragmatic breath... expand your abdomen... and let it fall. You cannot stop the initial flash of performance anxiety. When intimacy approaches, your brain will likely trigger a wave of adrenaline. Your job is not to prevent the wave. Your job is to stop bracing against it.

When you feel that familiar rush of nervous energy in your chest, do not panic about the panic. Recognize it simply as a misplaced autonomic alarm system. Say to yourself, 'There is the adrenaline.' Then, consciously execute a Pelvic Drop and breathe slow. Let the adrenaline wash pass through you. It only stays locked in your body if you try to fight it.
