Welcome to Day 34... <break time="1.5s" /> As you expose yourself to higher levels of arousal, the spectator will try to return. Intrusive thoughts will pop up: 'I'm taking too long,' or 'I'm going to lose it.'

In behavioral therapy, we use Cognitive Defusion. You are not your thoughts. A thought is just a piece of neurochemical static passing through your brain. You do not have to believe it, and you do not have to fight it. When an anxious thought arises, simply label it. Say to yourself, 'There is an anxious thought.' Then, immediately return your focus to the physical sensation of your breath. Let the static pass.
