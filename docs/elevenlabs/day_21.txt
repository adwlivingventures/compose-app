Clinically, this is disastrous for an anxious man. Sprinting to a 9 trains your nervous system to stay in a state of high-alert panic, and it creates massive hypertonicity in your pelvic floor.

COMPOSE does not teach edging. We teach plateauing. Your goal is not to flirt with the edge of the cliff. Your goal is to comfortably set up camp at a level 6 or 7. Smooth, controlled, and relaxed. Stop sprinting to the edge. Learn to master the middle.
