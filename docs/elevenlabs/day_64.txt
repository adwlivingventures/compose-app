Welcome to Day 64. Take a deep, grounded breath. Let's look at the cranial nerves. The nerves that control your eye contact and facial expressions are directly tied to the Ventral Vagal complex—the safety center of your nervous system.

When you make soft, sustained eye contact with a partner, you are mechanically stimulating the vagus nerve, which pumps the brakes on your heart rate and your adrenaline production. If the physical heat is getting too high today, stop moving, open your eyes, and look into hers. Use the neurobiology of connection to override the neurobiology of panic.
