Welcome to Day 24. Settle in and listen closely. You are rewiring years, perhaps decades, of autonomic conditioning. There will be days when the spectator returns. There will be encounters where the adrenaline wins, you finish faster than you wanted, or your body refuses to respond.

When this happens, your inner critic will try to tear you apart. It will tell you that the protocol isn't working and that you are broken. Do not listen to it. A falter is not a relapse; it is simply data. It is your body telling you that the stress load was a little too high that day.

Forgive the falter. Take a breath. Tomorrow is a new baseline. Progress is never a straight line—it is a rising tide.
