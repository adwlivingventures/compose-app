Welcome to Day 36... <break time="1.5s" /> Settle in and breathe deep. Over years of solo habits, your body became hardwired to respond to one very specific angle, pressure, and speed of touch. Real intimacy does not look like that. It is unpredictable.

When a partner's touch feels different than what you are used to, your brain often panics and drops your arousal. You must forgive the friction. If the angle isn't perfect, do not retreat into your head to worry about it. Stay in your body. Allow your nervous system to learn a new vocabulary of touch. Adaptability is the core of true stamina.
