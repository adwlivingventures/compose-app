Rewire v2 — what changed & why

Interactive mockup — press and hold the old script, then run the spike flow. Three instruments, one narrative: scheduled rep → incident tool → accumulating proof.

1 · Hold-to-cross-out

The rewire becomes a motor act, not a read. Pressing and holding while the old belief gets struck through is embodied defusion — the generation effect plus a physical commitment gesture. A tap is ignorable; a 1.2-second hold is a decision.

The rep counter ("Rep 34 of 75") reframes the card from content-of-the-day to a training set with visible accumulation — the same endowment mechanic as the progress ring.

2 · Tag first, counter delivered

Spec-correct: §5 promises an immediate pre-written reframe. A user mid-spike is sympathetically activated — impaired prefrontal access — and cannot author a good reframe. So v2 asks him only to recognize (tap a pattern card), then delivers the authored clinical counter. Writing is optional, after down-regulation.

The closing exhale pairs the cognitive counter with a vagal brake — polyvagal state change, not just insight. Sixty seconds, ends calmer than it began.

3 · The evidence locker

History stops being a list and becomes an asset: dominant-pattern naming ("it's one known voice, not fourteen problems") is trait-level defusion; the declining spike curve is the extinction evidence that feeds Day-14, graduation, and the month-11 renewal-evidence screen.

"Days since last spike" is the quiet flex metric — absence of pathology made visible.
